# Week 4 Reading and EDGAR Examples

## 1. Greenblatt, Chapter 3

Many institutional holders sell the spinoff in its first months: some index funds sell because it is outside their index, and some funds sell because it is too small or does not fit their mandate. This selling comes from their rules, not from what the business is worth.

## 2. Klarman, Chapter 10

The parent can be the cheap piece when holders sell it before the spin to buy the new company, or sell the leftover parent after the spin, so its price drops for reasons that have nothing to do with its value.

## 3. EDGAR examples

### Spinoff: Honeywell Aerospace Inc. (CIK 2089271)

- **Registration filing:** Form 10-12B/A, filed June 8, 2026, accession 0001628280-26-041399 ([SEC filing index](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2089271/000162828026041399/0001628280-26-041399-index.htm)).
- **Information statement:** Exhibit 99.1 to a Form 8-K filed June 15, 2026, accession 0001628280-26-043193 ([SEC filing index and exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2089271/000162828026043193/0001628280-26-043193-index.htm)).
- **Parent:** Honeywell International Inc.
- **Distribution ratio:** 1 Aerospace share for every 2 Honeywell shares.
- **Dates:** Record date June 15, 2026; distribution date June 29, 2026.
- **What the parent leaves in the new company:**
  - Debt: Aerospace issued $16.0B in notes and had $4.0B in credit facilities.
  - Planned: a $4.0B commercial paper program.
  - What Honeywell received: $6.0B in Exchange Notes and $9.1B in cash from other notes, as partial payment for the assets it contributed.
  - Retained stake: none.
- **Pro forma financials (unaudited estimates, reviewed):**
  - Net income for 2025 was $2.722B historical and $1.815B pro forma. The adjustments include $839M of interest on the new debt and a $285M autonomous-entity SG&A adjustment, among others.
  - On the unaudited pro forma balance sheet as of March 28, 2026: $1.0B cash, $15.846B long-term debt, and a $3.454B total equity deficit.
  - The filing warns that final amounts after the distribution could differ.

### Not a spinoff: 5C Lending Partners Corp. (CIK 1998387)

- **Filing:** Form 10-12G, filed July 1, 2024, accession 0000930413-24-001987 ([SEC filing index](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1998387/0000930413-24-001987-index.htm)).
- **Why it is not a spinoff:** It registered in connection with its election to be a business development company and a private offering, so no parent is distributing shares.
