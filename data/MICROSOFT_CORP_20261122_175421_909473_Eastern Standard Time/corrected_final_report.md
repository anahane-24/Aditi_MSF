Insight: The 10-K says ongoing investment in cloud and AI infrastructure will increase operating costs and may decrease operating margins (10-K_2026-07-29.txt, line 1550, accession 0001193125-26-323660).

Microsoft reports that FY2026 Microsoft Cloud revenue increased 27% to $214.4 billion. Azure and other cloud services revenue increased 41%. Commercial remaining performance obligation increased 84% to $678 billion (10-K_2026-07-29.txt, lines 1532–1538; accession 0001193125-26-323660). These are company-reported growth measures. The company describes its markets as dynamic and highly competitive. It also identifies datacenter power, land, networking, and server availability as operating dependencies (10-K_2026-07-29.txt, lines 1543, 1549–1550; accession 0001193125-26-323660). Microsoft says it extended its OpenAI partnership in October 2025 and April 2026 (same 10-K, line 1544).
