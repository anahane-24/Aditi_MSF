## Overview

Microsoft operates as a technology company committed to making digital technology and artificial intelligence (AI) available broadly and responsibly, with a mission to empower every person and every organization on the planet to achieve more. The company develops and supports a broad portfolio of technology solutions for individuals and businesses, focusing on secure, trusted, and innovative platforms and applications that meet evolving customer needs across AI, cloud computing, productivity and collaboration, and personal computing.

Microsoft's business model is structured around three reportable segments: **Productivity and Business Processes**, **Intelligent Cloud**, and **More Personal Computing**. These segments provide management with a comprehensive financial view of key businesses and enable alignment of strategies and objectives across development, sales, marketing, and services organizations.

- **Productivity and Business Processes** includes Microsoft 365 Commercial and Consumer products and cloud services, LinkedIn, and Dynamics products and cloud services.
- **Intelligent Cloud** encompasses server products and cloud services (including Azure), and enterprise and partner services.
- **More Personal Computing** includes Windows and Devices, XBOX (formerly Gaming), and Search advertising.

## Market Positioning and Growth Outlook

Microsoft's competitive advantages stem from its integrated AI-driven products addressing security, compliance, identity, management, and privacy across multi-cloud, application, and device assets. The company's strategic ambitions include reinventing productivity and business processes, building an intelligent cloud and edge platform, and creating more personal computing experiences.
