## Overview

Costco operates membership warehouses and e-commerce sites. Its stated model uses low prices, a limited product selection, high sales volumes, rapid inventory turnover, volume purchasing, and efficient distribution. Costco says this model supports lower gross margins than many retailers (10-K_2026-10-07.txt, line 139; accession 0000909832-26-000093). Its strategy is to offer high-quality merchandise at prices it believes are consistently low. It carries fewer than 4,000 active SKUs in a warehouse and averages 9,000 to 11,000 online SKUs (same 10-K, line 146).

## Market Positioning and Growth Outlook

Costco reported fiscal 2026 total revenue of $303.154 billion, up 10.1437% from $275.235 billion in fiscal 2025. Membership fee revenue rose 11% to $5.907 billion. Costco reported U.S. and Canada renewal rates of 92.3% and worldwide renewal rates of 89.8% at fiscal year end (10-K_2026-10-07.txt, lines 638, 464-468; accession 0000909832-26-000093). These are historical company results. They do not establish that the growth or renewal rates will continue.

Comparable sales for the 16-week fourth quarter rose 9.4%, or 6.7% after the company's stated adjustment for gasoline prices and foreign exchange. Digitally-enabled comparable sales rose 19.5% (8-K_Ex99.1_2026-09-24.txt, lines 20-23; accession 0000909832-26-000084). Costco reported 939 warehouses at the time of the release (same 8-K, line 25).

The company reported that gross margin percentage fell 3 basis points to 11.09% for fiscal 2026. It said tariff refunds, net of reinvestment in member value, increased gross margin by 3 basis points (10-K_2026-10-07.txt, lines 474-475). The 8-K says the fourth-quarter net income included a one-time benefit of $0.15 per diluted share from IEEPA tariff refunds, less partial reinvestment in member value (8-K_Ex99.1_2026-09-24.txt, line 24). Treat this as a non-recurring item, based on the company's wording.

Costco lists competition, inflation or deflation, consumer spending, wage and health-care costs, tariffs, energy, and real-estate conditions among the risks that may affect results (8-K_Ex99.1_2026-09-24.txt, line 29). These are company risk disclosures, not forecasts of a specific outcome.
